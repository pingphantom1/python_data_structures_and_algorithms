The Challenge: The E-Commerce Price Tag Sorter 🛒

Imagine you are building a backend feature for an online store. Customers want to browse products sorted from the lowest price to the highest price.

The Starting Data:
catalog = [
    {'item': 'Wireless Mouse', 'price': 29.99},
    {'item': 'Mechanical Keyboard', 'price': 89.50},
    {'item': 'USB-C Cable', 'price': 12.00},
    {'item': 'Gaming Monitor', 'price': 249.99},
    {'item': 'Desk Lamp', 'price': 45.00}
]

Your Task:

Write a Python function using Selection Sort from scratch to sort this list of dictionaries by their price in ascending order (lowest to highest).

Expected output

[
    {'item': 'USB-C Cable', 'price': 12.00},
    {'item': 'Wireless Mouse', 'price': 29.99},
    {'item': 'Desk Lamp', 'price': 45.00},
    {'item': 'Mechanical Keyboard', 'price': 89.50},
    {'item': 'Gaming Monitor', 'price': 249.99}
]
